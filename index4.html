GOVERNMENT OF KERALA

The Government of Kerala was formed on 1 November 1956, following the reorganisation of states on a linguistic basis. Kerala has a three-tier Panchayati Raj system to strengthen local self-government and promote people's participation in development. The system consists of Grama Panchayats at the village level, Block Panchayats at the intermediate level, and District Panchayats at the district level. Grama Panchayats administer local areas and address the basic needs and development requirements of local communities. Panchayats play an important role in decentralised governance and rural development in Kerala.

MELILA GRAMA PANCHAYAT

Melila Grama Panchayat is located in Kollam district, Kerala. This WebGIS project presents geographical and administrative information about the Panchayat, including its location, boundaries, wards, roads, resource distribution and other important features. The project helps users explore the geographical characteristics of the Panchayat.
